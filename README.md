## Featured project: [Margin](https://github.com/huzexe/margin)

**A study-notes app that turns rough lecture notes into clean, organized study material.**

You type short notes while you listen. Margin turns them into structured notes with definitions, formulas, worked examples, diagrams, key takeaways and flashcards, then exports a PDF with a table of contents.

- **Library:** subjects, tags, full-text search and autosave
- **Enhance notes:** per-section regenerate, with a 15-second undo
- **Diagrams:** Mermaid diagrams with an edit panel and full-screen zoom
- **PDF export:** headless Chrome/Edge with real page numbers in the table of contents
- **Backup and restore,** a one-command installer, and optional phone access over home Wi-Fi
- Runs locally on Linux and Windows, and your notes stay on your machine

`Python` `FastAPI` `SQLite (FTS5)` `JavaScript` `Editor.js` `KaTeX` `Mermaid` `Playwright` `Gemini API`

## Team project: [Rocketrix Ground Station](https://github.com/huzexe/Rocketrix-GroundStation-Demo)

I'm a team member on the ground station software for our Level 1 high-power rocket. The project is led by [Mohid](https://github.com/M0hid-ai/Rocketrix-GroundStation-Demo).

- Live LoRa telemetry dashboard with altitude, velocity, acceleration and link health
- Kalman-filtered altitude that fuses barometer and IMU data
- Flight-phase tracking (pad, boost, coast, apogee, drogue, main, landed)
- Automatic post-flight reports with CSV export
- A full physics simulation that sends the same binary packets as the ESP32 flight computer, so the whole pipeline can be tested before the hardware flies

`Python` `FastAPI` `WebSockets` `React` `uPlot` `ESP32` `LoRa`
